Restringing Service

Strings lose tension and feel over time, especially if you play regularly. A fresh string job can improve control, power, and comfort. Many players benefit from restringing at least as many times per year as they play per week, or any time their racket starts to feel “dead.”

Whether you are a current student or a local player looking for a quick, reliable string job, Coach DWag offers a convenient restringing service right here in Rexburg.

Pricing & Turnaround

Pricing depends on the type of string you choose and whether you are a current coaching student. Below are typical options and time frames.

Pricing

Current students: discounted rates are available.

  • Standard synthetic gut: about $20–$25 (string + labor)
  • Comfort / arm-friendly setup: about $25–$30
  • Control / spin setup or hybrid: about $30–$35
  • Labor only (you bring the string): about $15–$20

Exact pricing can be confirmed when you contact Coach Dallin and choose your string type.

Turnaround Time

Most rackets are finished within 1–2 days, depending on schedule and string availability.

  • Rush jobs may be available before matches or tournaments.
  • Please mention important match dates when you schedule.
  • You will be notified when your racket is ready for pickup.

The goal is to keep you on court with as little downtime as possible.

How to Drop Off & Which String to Choose

Rackets can be dropped off in Rexburg at a location shared when you schedule your restringing. Please remove any dampeners or accessories you want to keep, and clearly label your racket if more than one belongs to your family.

Which String Should I Choose?

The right string depends on your level, arm health, and playing style. Here are some general guidelines:

  • Beginners / casual players: standard synthetic gut for a balanced feel.
  • Players with arm or shoulder concerns: softer, comfort-focused strings.
  • Competitive players: polyester or hybrid setups for more control and spin.

If you are not sure what to pick, Coach DWag can recommend a setup based on how often you play and what you want to improve.
