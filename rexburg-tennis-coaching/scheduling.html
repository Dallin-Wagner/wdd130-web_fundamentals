Lessons & Clinics

We offer private lessons, small group sessions, and focused clinics on serving, net play, and match strategy. Each option is designed to help you improve in a structured but fun environment.

Private Lessons

  • 1-on-1 instruction
  • Ideal for focused technical work
  • Great for all levels

Typical rate: $30–$40 per hour (contact for current pricing)

Small Group Sessions

  • 2–4 players per court
  • Live-ball drills and point play
  • Work on consistency and movement

Typical rate: $15–$20 per player per hour

Specialty Clinics

  • Serve & return
  • Net play and volleys
  • Match strategy and mental toughness

Schedule: Offered seasonally. See sign-up form for current dates.
