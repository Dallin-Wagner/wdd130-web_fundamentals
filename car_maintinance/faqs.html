Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most often.

How often should I change my oil?

For most modern vehicles, every 5,000–7,500 miles is a good rule of thumb, but we always check your manufacturer’s recommendations and your driving habits.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome for quick checks, but an appointment guarantees a faster visit and lets us reserve a bay just for you.

Will you do any work without my approval?

Never. We explain what we found, what’s urgent, what can wait, and give you pricing before any work is done.

Can you work with my vehicle’s warranty?

Yes. We follow manufacturer maintenance schedules and keep detailed records so your warranty stays intact.

How do I know what services my car needs next?

Your AutoCare dashboard (and the home page of this site) shows upcoming mileage-based services, due dates, and recommended inspections.

What if I have questions after my visit?

Give us a call or send an email — we’re happy to re-explain anything from your visit or go over your next steps again.

Still need help?

Reach out and we’ll walk through your situation one-on-one.
